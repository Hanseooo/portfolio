# Content Strategy

## Recommended Messaging Hierarchy
The narrative flow should guide the visitor from high-level identity down to concrete proof:
1. **The Hook (Hero):** Who you are and the unique value you bring at a glance.
2. **The Philosophy (Approach):** How you think about engineering and product architecture.
3. **The Proof (Selected Work):** Deep dive into 3 featured projects that validate your philosophy.
4. **The Trajectory (Experience):** Your professional timeline and impact.
5. **The Trust (Credentials/Contact):** Validation and clear paths to engagement.

## Suggested Headline Options for Hero
The current "Full-Stack Engineer focused on AI Product Engineering" is accurate but lacks punch.
*Options:*
- "Engineering with Intent. Building intelligent systems with cinematic precision."
- "Architecting clarity from complexity. Full-Stack & AI Product Engineer."
- "Building systems that scale. Designing experiences that resonate."

## Section-by-Section Copy Direction

### 01: Hero / Identity
- **Keep:** Focus on "System design", "AI-enabled workflows", and "clean implementation."
- **Rewrite:** Elevate the language to be more assertive. E.g., instead of "I build maintainable systems...", use "Architecting maintainable systems with uncompromising precision."
- **Remove:** Overly generic phrases that apply to all junior developers.

### 02: Approach (Formerly "Skills")
- **Keep:** The 3 skill groups concept (Product Engineering, Systems Thinking, Delivery Workflow).
- **Rewrite:** Frame these as "Methodologies" or "Core Disciplines." Compress the tool list ("The Stack I Trust") into a sleek, non-intrusive component (like a scrolling ticker or a subtle grid) rather than a bulleted list. 
- **Compress:** "19 core technologies" should be visually grouped (e.g., Core, Data, AI) rather than listed individually in text.

### 03: Selected Work
- **Keep:** SimplyNote, The Podium, Lost and Found System.
- **Rewrite:** Shift focus from *what* it is to *why* it matters and *how* it was built. Include specific metrics or architectural decisions.
- **Remove:** Long descriptions on the homepage. Keep only a sharp 1-liner and a strong visual.
- **Action:** Move full details to a dedicated `/projects` subpage.

### 04: Experience (Timeline)
- **Keep:** Eskwelabs and Freelance roles.
- **Rewrite:** Emphasize the 80% workload reduction metric at Eskwelabs. Focus on "Shipped X resulting in Y."
- **Compress:** On the homepage, show a streamlined visual timeline. Link to an `/experience` subpage for full bullet points.

### 05: Credentials & Contact
- **Keep:** Cisco and Codechum certs, Live Signals.
- **Rewrite:** Group certs elegantly. Make "Live Signals" a sleek footer widget rather than a main section.
- **Action:** Move full cert list to `/certificates`. Keep only 1-2 featured ones on the homepage or rely on the subpage entirely.
