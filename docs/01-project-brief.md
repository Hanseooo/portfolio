# Project Brief: Hanseo.tech Redesign

## Project Goal
To overhaul the current portfolio website (https://www.hanseo.tech/) into a premium, cinematic, and story-driven experience. The redesign will elevate the presentation from a standard developer portfolio to a high-end, editorial showcase of a senior full-stack and AI product engineer.

## Problem Statement
While the current site effectively lists skills and projects, it lacks a cohesive narrative and the "wow" factor expected of a senior-level engineer or founder. It needs to reflect a higher standard of frontend architecture and UX design, acting as undeniable proof of the builder's capabilities rather than just a list of them.

## Design Inspiration Summary
The redesign draws structural and pacing inspiration from the official Charles Leclerc website (https://charlesleclerc.com/en/). Key takeaways include:
- **Chapter-based storytelling:** Organizing content into distinct, numbered phases (e.g., 01 Identity, 02 Approach, 03 Execution).
- **Cinematic pacing:** Generous spacing, large immersive sections, and scroll-triggered pacing that forces the user to digest one concept at a time.
- **Editorial typography & layout:** Large, confident headers combined with asymmetric layouts and high-contrast styling.

## Identity Constraints
- **Theme:** Unmistakably engineering and tech-focused. No automotive, racing, or Ferrari branding.
- **Color Palette:** Deep black base with "Ice Blue" accents.
- **Tone:** Sharp, technical, confident, yet human.
- **Focus:** System design, full-stack architecture, and AI-enabled product engineering.

## Success Criteria
1. **Visual Excellence:** The site feels undeniably premium, modern, and memorable.
2. **Clear Narrative:** Visitors immediately understand the unique value proposition (AI + Full-Stack Architecture).
3. **Flawless Execution:** Buttery smooth animations, zero jank on scroll, and immediate load times.
4. **Responsive Integrity:** The cinematic feel translates effectively to mobile devices without breaking layouts.
5. **Accessibility:** Passes WCAG standards for contrast and keyboard navigation, with support for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
